# Brag Plan: Plugg (Mobile View Ad Campaign)

## What is this app?
Plugg is an independent African music discovery and direct artist distribution platform engineered for East Africa and beyond, merging a sleek, dark-mode mobile web player with direct-to-fan artist publishing and streaming telemetry.

## The angle
A high-energy, vertical mobile video ad campaign (9:16) built specifically for mobile screens, social feeds, and digital ad channels. It showcases Plugg as the category-defining mobile music app for independent African creators and listeners — spotlighting Sonet's hit single *Forever* (Nebbi, Westnile, Uganda) and demonstrating seamless mobile streaming, live telemetry, and zero-middlemen distribution straight from a phone.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark obsidian void framed by an electric vertical smartphone outline with pulsing audio-reactive energy.
Big kinetic typography lands with punch:
"YOUR SOUND."
"YOUR STREAM."
"YOUR POCKET."
Pill badge: "EAST AFRICA • NEBBI • WESTNILE • UGANDA"

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Plugg Mobile Player in Action (Scene 2 — 4.2s–9.2s):** A photorealistic smartphone screen showcasing Plugg's mobile interface. Viewers see the mobile discovery feed featuring Sonet's *Forever*, followed by an interactive tap on the floating mobile player bar that expands into the full-bleed player with glowing waveform bars, real-time scrubber, and verified artist certification.
- **Direct Mobile Artist Telemetry (Scene 3 — 9.2s–14.5s):** The creator power experience on mobile — three high-impact glassmorphic telemetry cards cascade onto the mobile viewport:
  1. "8,420,330" — Total Streams (Uganda & Global)
  2. "312,480" — Monthly Listeners
  3. "VERIFIED ARTIST" — Direct Royalties & Mobile Uploads

## Outro / punchline
Monumental mobile brand lock:
"PLUGG."
"STREAM. PUBLISH. AMPLIFY."
Action CTA:
"TAP TO PLAY"
"plugg.site"
"Nebbi • Westnile • Uganda • East Africa"

## User flow worth showing
1. Discovering Sonet (*Forever*, Nebbi, Westnile, Uganda) on the mobile feed.
2. Tapping the floating mini player bar to open the full mobile player with animated soundwave telemetry.
3. Swiping into the artist dashboard displaying real-time stream counts and zero-middlemen payouts.
4. Call-to-action to stream instantly on mobile at plugg.site.

- Creative direction: "high-energy mobile launch ad campaign for independent African sound"
- Interpretation: Fast-paced vertical mobile pacing, ultra-high contrast dark-mode surfaces, glassmorphic card borders, crisp mobile typography, and trailer-scale punch.

## Audio direction
- Role: Driving afrobeat / modern trailer bed with crisp mobile UI feedback and punchy punctuation.
- Music: `assets/music/forever-by-sonet.mp3` (Sonet - Forever)
- Music treatment: Plays from 0.0s to 19.0s at volume 0.45, with a smooth 1.5s fade-out at the finish.
- Music cue guidance:
  - Strong hits at ~4.2s (scene switch to mobile player), ~9.2s (telemetry reveal), ~15.6s (brand slam).
  - Beat-grid windows for metric cards at 10.2s, 11.2s, 12.2s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: Subtle ambient radial glow behind the mobile phone mockup that breathes with music RMS.
- SFX posture: Motion-matched mobile UI sound design — bass impact at hook, crisp tap click on play, smooth card drops, and resonant bell on brand resolution.
- Restraint rule: Keep SFX tight and grounded in UI gestures rather than cartoonish noise.

- **Transition mood:** Zoom-through into the phone screen → Scene 2
